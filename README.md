## 📢 Important Note on Textbooks Availability

> [!NOTE]
> **Digital Textbooks in Semesters 5 & 6:**  
> In Semesters 5 and 6, only **Management** textbooks (`DMGT206`, `DMGT208`) are issued digitally by the university. Non-management textbooks (advanced English, Punjabi literature, and Sociology) are physical curriculum purchases from the LPU University bookstore. All available digital study notes for these subjects are indexed below!

## 🎓 Official 24-Course Curriculum Overview

This repository and the web portal conform strictly to the official LPU Distance Education B.A. curriculum structure (4 subjects per semester, 24 courses total):

| Semester | Course 1 | Course 2 | Course 3 | Course 4 |
| :---: | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Sem 4** | `DSOC202` Social Stratification | `DPBI201` Punjabi-II | `DMGT207` Management of Finances | `DENG203` Elective English-IV |
| **Sem 5** | `DENG302` Elective English-V | `DENG301` English-III | `DMGT206` Production and Operations Management | `DSOC301` Society in India |
| **Sem 6** | `DSOC302` Disorganization and Emerging Problems | `DENG303` Elective English-VI | `DMGT208` Human Resource Management | `DPBI301` Punjabi-III |
