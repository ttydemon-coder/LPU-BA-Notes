## 📢 Important Note on Textbooks Availability

> [!NOTE]
> **Digital Textbooks in Semesters 5 & 6:**  
> In Semesters 5 and 6, only **Management** textbooks (`DMGT206`, `DMGT208`) are issued digitally by the university. Non-management textbooks (advanced English, Punjabi literature, and Sociology) are physical curriculum purchases only from the LPU University bookstore.

---

## 🔍 Core Subjects Covered

This repository focuses strictly on the 4 primary B.A. degree tracks:

1. **English** (`DENG103`, `DENG104`, `DENG105`, `DENG201`, `DENG202`, `DENG203`, `DENG301`, `DENG302`, `DENG303`)
2. **Management** (`DMGT101`, `DMGT203`, `DMGT204`, `DMGT206`, `DMGT207`, `DMGT208`)
3. **Punjabi** (`DPBI101`, `DPBI201`, `DPBI301`)
4. **Social Science / Sociology** (`DSOC101`, `DSOC102`, `DSOC201`, `DSOC202`, `DSOC301`, `DSOC302`)

To download all 61 PDF study materials to your computer or phone at once:
